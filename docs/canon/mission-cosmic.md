# Mission Cosmic

## Status

Mission Cosmic identity direction is considered canonically stabilized.

Mission Cosmic inherits the same foundational operational topology language as Mission Dark and Mission Light, but expands the ecosystem into multidimensional recursive awareness, dimensional resonance, and infinite operational continuity.

Mission Cosmic is not a generic sci-fi mode, galaxy wallpaper mode, fantasy abstraction, or psychedelic chaos layer.

It is the transcendent operational state of the same recursive intelligence civilization.
